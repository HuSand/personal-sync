Personal Sync

Personal Sync is a private, single-user tool. It runs on its owner's own computer and helps them keep track of their own finances and health data.

  • Gmail (read-only): reads the owner's own bank transaction notification emails to record spending.
  • Google Drive (read-only): reads health export files that the owner saves to their own Drive.

The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by its owner.
